Privacy Policy — draft

This informational draft is awaiting review by Ramdani Law Firm. It is not a finalised privacy notice.

Who we are

Ramdani Law Firm, Dera Ghazi Khan, Pakistan, operates this public website. Contact details beyond city and country are published only when the firm supplies them.

What we collect

If you use a public form we store the name, email, phone, subject, and message you submit, plus basic technical logs needed to run the site. Do not send privileged documents through these forms.

Why we collect it

To respond to consultation, contact, internship, or book-inquiry requests. Form data is not used to create a lawyer–client relationship by itself.

Pakistani context

We process form data because you submitted it to be contacted. This draft is not a claim of GDPR establishment in the EU. If the firm later offers services to persons in other jurisdictions, this policy must be reviewed.

Cookies

The site may store a language or theme preference in your browser. We do not run a paid advertising pixel on these pages.

Sharing

Hosting, email, and database providers process data to run the site. We do not sell form submissions.

Retention

Inquiry records are kept as long as needed to respond and for ordinary professional record-keeping, then deleted or archived according to staff policy.

Your requests

To ask what we hold about a form you submitted, use the contact page. Identity checks may be required before any disclosure.
